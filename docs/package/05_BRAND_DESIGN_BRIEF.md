# Brand and Design Brief — Touchform.md

## Status
Use **Touchform.md** as the project name per the founder's latest instruction. Do not silently rebrand. The logo and final visual identity are still to be designed: show three strategic alternatives, test them at realistic scales and request founder approval before public production use. As a pre-launch risk gate, verify name/domain/trademark and current market-use conflicts; report evidence rather than assuming legal availability.

## Intended impression
A locally grounded but globally credible specialist; inventive, exact, tactile, confident, and useful. More like a product design studio + interaction lab than a low-cost gadget shop.

## Visual principles
- Clear typographic hierarchy and calm layouts; use whitespace intentionally.
- Distinctive product imagery that shows actual materials, dimensions and scale.
- Product detail and tactile texture paired with a clean, precise digital interface.
- The hero can be cinematic, but the rest of the UI must remain fast, scannable and conversion-oriented.
- Do not make every section a rounded card. Use varied composition, large editorial product shots and controlled grids.
- Keep accessible contrast, reduced-motion support, keyboard usability and large mobile tap targets.
- Do not use a generic AI-generated neon gradient or a stock NFC chip icon as the whole identity.

## Logo exploration
Create three genuinely different strategic directions, not three cosmetic variations:
1. **Threshold/portal:** a visual passage from physical to digital.
2. **Connection/bridge:** two forms becoming one system.
3. **Typographic signature:** distinctive Touchform wordmark with one ownable altered letterform or ligature.

Each direction must include rationale, wordmark, icon, monochrome version, favicon-sized test, and mock application on a real stand. The standalone mark must work at tiny sizes, in monochrome, across white/black/brown/blue/yellow or brand-specific product colors, and in single-color printing/engraving. Test visual distinction from local competitors; do not imitate Touchform-like existing market assets or Google identity.

## Product imagery and motion
- Custom product generator prioritizes the object itself: front/three-quarter view, visible shape, color/material and branding, simple background.
- Available stand colors can include white, black, brown/wood, blue, yellow and specific brand colors, but only use these as visual options after matching them to validated production options.
- Hero concept: the same identical stand remains in the same scale, position, angle and design while surroundings shift between cafe/bar, reception, office and other contexts. Use careful match cuts or foreground compositing. If an AI video cannot keep the product invariant, use matched stills/crossfades or real footage instead.
- Video has a compressed poster and reduced-motion/static fallback. No autoplay sound. Content and CTA load independently of the video.

## UI behavior
- Standard product: accurate stateful live preview, consistent option controls and transparent price.
- AI consultant: conversational discovery, proactive useful ideas and personalized textual offer. Avoid making it look like a generic chatbot plugin.
- AI Concept Studio: spacious workspace with brief/offer and product concept/phone demo; all generated outputs read from one canonical JSON contract.
- Use state labels for estimated / verified / needs human review.
- Mobile AI flow should keep idea, concept and CTA simple; avoid a multi-column desktop layout squeezed onto a phone.
